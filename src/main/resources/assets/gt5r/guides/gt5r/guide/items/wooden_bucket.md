# Wooden Bucket

<ItemImage id="wooden_bucket" scale="5" />

## Description
The Wooden Bucket is an early game bucket that you can use for early game before you can easily produce iron.  Can only hold water for now, nothing else. I may in the future extend it to hold more fluids as long as they are not too hot.

## Usage
The Wooden Bucket works like the iron one.  This bucket can be used to put water in a [Solid Fuel Boiler](../steam/solid_fuel_burner.md) to generate steam or even a Cauldron so you can wash your impure ores.
