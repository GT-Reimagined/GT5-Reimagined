# What is GT5 Reimagined?

To explain that, first lets go over the histroy of GregTech 5(GT5 for short). Back in 2014 or 2015 Greg created GT5, 
and people liked it. But Greg was not satisfied with GT5, so he abandoned it and started making GregTech 6 instead. 
After Greg abandoned it, someone else named BloodAsp(aka Bloody) came along and decided to Continue GT5 as GT5 Unofficial. 
He first started by making GT5.08, Which mainly just tried to flesh out the missing endgame from GT5 with some 
things grabbed from GT4 and modified to fit in GT5. After that, Bloody then moved on to making GT5.09, 
which changed a lot of things to make them harder, like circuits and oil processing. If you have ever played GregTech, it's likely
that GT509 is the version you are most familiar with.

As for what this is, essentially it's my take on where GT5 could have gone if Greg hadn't abandoned it while still keeping 
the good features from GT5U. To Of course I'm not Greg, so I can't really say how he would've done it, but I have tried to make 
things fit more in his style, which is one reason I've added all the nice QOL features from GT6. Plus they're just really useful, 
so how could I not? Of course this mod will still be mostly like GT5U, but some processing chains will be changed, and there won't 
be some of the dumb BS from GT509.
