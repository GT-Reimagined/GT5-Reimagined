## Description
Red Granite and Black Granite tools are similar to regular [Stone Tools](./stone_tools.md) where they have low durability.  The advantage of Red and Black Granite tools are they are Mining Level 3 tools.  This allows them to actually mine through Red and Black Granite layers.  This allows for the player to actually get down to diamond levels without needing tier 3 metal tools.

## Usage
The Read and Black Granite tools have both a rock and cobblestone crafting recipes.  Both recipes only require 1 stick and the appropriate number of red or black granite rocks or cobblestone blocks.  The recipes require the same type, so you cannot mix and match.
