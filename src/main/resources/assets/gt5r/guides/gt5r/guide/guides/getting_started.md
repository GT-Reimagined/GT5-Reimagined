# Getting Started in GT5r

## After Spawning - First Day
Just like any first day in minecraft, you will needsome basic stuff to start crafting and to find a safe place to spend your first night.
You will notice that you will see a bunch of stones on the ground.  You can pick the rocks without any ores and use them to make your
first basic tools.  You you don't feel like punching trees to get some wood logs, you can make a stone axe with 3 stones of the same 
type and a stick. You cna get sticks from punching leaves.  The stone tools have very low durability but will get you going until you
can make flint tools or get your first ores.  While getting started, you can start marking the ore veins at the location you see ore rocks
on the surface.  The rocks will indicate that an ore block is somewhere below that surface rock.  You can make a knife as your first weapon 
by combinding flint and a stick in the user crafting grid.  This weapon will also have a chance to ignite a mob on fire when attacking them.  
Finally find a safe place to spend the night.
  
---
  
## Ores to Look Out for
While scanning the surface, you will want to look out for [Tetrahedrite/Copper, Cassiterite/Tin, and Galena/Lead](../alloys/copper_tin_lead.md).  At this point you will want to
mine small ores as they will give you crushed or impute ores that you can cook in a Minecraft furnace.  It is also best to only cook the pure copper, 
tin, and lead ores and save the complex ores for when you have more advanced processes to get the byproducts out of the ores.  Also make sure to be on 
the lookout for coal rocks as they will indicate a Andersite or lignite Coal layer below.  Mining any regular ore blocks will give you raw ores which will 
need further processing.  You can burn raw coal in the minecraft furnace.  You will also find many small ore in sand (i.e. Desert/Beach biomes) so 
make sure to pick these up on your travels.

##  Advancing Further
You will want to find a place for your base.  You will want to look for a river or ocean biome as you will need water.  Unlike regular Minecraft, infinite 
water has been disabled so picking up water with buckets will deplete it.  Habing a drain connected to pipes will give you infinite water but it has to be 
done in a river or ocean biome.  Connecting your pipe to other 
water sources that are not in a river or ocean bione, those water sources will be drained dry.  Continue working to gather copper and tin so you can make 
your first bronze.  Once you have bronze you can make your first [Solid Fuel Boiler (Bronze)](../steam/solid_fuel_burner.md) which will advance you 
into the Bronze Steam age.
