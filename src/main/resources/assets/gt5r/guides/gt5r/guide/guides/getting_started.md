# Getting Started in GT5r

## After Spawning - First Day
Just like any first day in minecraft, you will need some basic stuff to start crafting and to find a safe place to spend your first night.
You will notice that you will see a bunch of stones on the ground.  You can pick the rocks without any ores and use them to make your
first basic tools.  If you don't feel like punching trees to get some wood logs, you can make a stone axe with 3 stones of the same 
type and a stick. You can get sticks from punching leaves.  The stone tools have very low durability but will get you going until you
can make flint tools or get your first ores.  While getting started, you can start marking the ore veins at the location you see ore rocks
on the surface.  The rocks will indicate that an ore block is somewhere below that surface rock.  You can make a knife as your first weapon 
by combining flint and a stick in the user crafting grid.  This weapon will also have a chance to ignite a mob on fire when attacking them.  
Finally find a safe place to spend the night.
  
---
  
## Ores to Look Out for
While scanning the surface, you will want to look out for rocks with [Tetrahedrite/Copper, Cassiterite/Tin, and Galena/Lead](../alloys/copper_tin_lead.md) ores.  At 
this point you will want to mine small ores as they will give you crushed or impure ores that you can cook in a Minecraft furnace.  It is best
to mine only the small ores early on so you can cook these ores into copper, tin, zinc, iron, and lead ingots.  Just save the larger ores, which produce raw ores, when 
you have more advanced processes available to get the byproducts out of the ores.  The larger full ores are also affected by fortune which will give more raw ores from 
a single block.  Also make sure to be on the lookout for coal rocks as they will indicate a Dense Coal or Lignite Coal layer below.  You can burn raw coal in the 
Minecraft furnace.  You will also find many small ores in sand and gravel areas (i.e. Desert/Beach biomes) so make sure to pick these up on your travels.

##  Advancing Further
You will want to find a place for your base.  You will want to look for a location with easy access to water as you will need water for your growing factory.  Depending on 
the mods installed you might have a finite water mod which will limit of the biomes you can draw water.  You will want to craft a drain and attach it to a water pipe and 
place it at least 1 block below the top water layer with at least a 3x3 area of water.  Installing the drain in a river or ocean biome will give you infinite water 
even if you have finite water mod.  The drain in a river or ocean biome will not call for block updates thus could reduce potential lag.  You should continue 
working to gather copper and tin so you can make your first bronze.  You also will need to look for surface native iron as you need 2 iron ingots in order to make a 
mortar.  The mortar will allow you to turn your copper and tin ingots into dusts so you can make bronze.  Once you have bronze you can make your first 
[Solid Fuel Boiler (Bronze)](../steam/solid_fuel_burner.md) which will advance you into the Bronze Steam age.
